# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want TRIaje 360 collaboration to be respectful, safe, evidence-oriented, and accessible to people across software, healthcare, research, design, and documentation backgrounds.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and evidence rather than people.
- State uncertainty and correct mistakes openly.
- Protect privacy and use synthetic data only.
- Respect professional scope, source licenses, and clinical governance.
- Welcome questions and make technical discussion accessible.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and avoid unsupported claims of certification, endorsement, or authority.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information, credentials, health data, or security details without permission.
- Knowingly fabricating test, clinical, regulatory, or research evidence.
- Pressuring contributors to work outside their professional competence.
- Using the project to provide individual diagnosis, prescriptions, or emergency advice.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to maintainers through the repository owner's GitHub contact method. Do not place sensitive evidence in a public issue. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants depending on severity and pattern.

This code applies in repository spaces and when someone represents the project in public.
